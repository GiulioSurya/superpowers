# Writing a criterion

Read this when the design gives different treatments to the members of a set: kinds of
data at an account deletion, session states at a reset, tools by how they load, endpoints
by who may call them.

A treatment decided member by member covers the list you were given. The members nobody
listed, and the ones added later, are then decided by whoever meets them, without your
human partner. A criterion decides every member, and one approval covers them all.

## What the criterion is

Prose, in your human partner's language, in a legal register: each word keeps one meaning
throughout, and the text leaves as little to interpretation as the subject allows. Its
parts, in this order:

1. **Definitions.** Every term the rule relies on, defined once.
2. **Categories.** The treatments, stated so that every member belongs to exactly one.
3. **The test.** One question that places any member in its category. Its answer is a fact
   about the member that anyone can check (what it holds, who reads it, what changes it),
   never an estimate of how much it matters.
4. **Consequences.** What happens to the members of each category.
5. **Exceptions.** Each one named, with its own rule. "Unless needed" or "where
   appropriate" is not an exception: it reopens the test.
6. **Completeness.** The design classifies every member of the set; a member left out is
   a defect of the design.

Examples stay out of the text; they go in the applications below it.

## How your human partner approves it

1. Send the criterion in a message of its own, with nothing else to decide in it. After
   it, two or three applications: the member the test places least obviously, and one
   whose treatment changes what exists today or what was said earlier.
2. Their approval covers that exact text. Any later change to it goes back to them.
3. Copy the approved text into the design doc verbatim, with the date of approval.
4. Apply the test to every member of the set, taken from a census of the project, not
   from the list you were given. The design carries the result as a table: member,
   category, the fact the test used. A member the test does not decide goes back to your
   human partner; it is never left for the plan.
